Drosera

A feedback drone synth. Drosera makes sound on its own: a noise floor feeds two string resonators inside a saturating loop, and you steer what the loop settles on.

This installer puts Drosera on this Mac for all users, in three formats:

  • VST3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • Standalone app in /Applications

To leave out a format, click Customize on the Installation Type step.
