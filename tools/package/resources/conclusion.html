Drosera is installed

Rescan plugins in your host. Drosera loads as an instrument. The Standalone app is in Applications.

Upgrading from 0.1.x

Earlier versions were copied by hand to your home folder. Delete ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Drosera.vst3, or your host finds two copies of Drosera.

Uninstalling

Delete these three items:

  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Drosera.vst3
  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Drosera.component
  • /Applications/Drosera.app

The standalone app also keeps its settings in ~/Library/Application Support/Drosera.settings.
